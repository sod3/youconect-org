# Bundled demo photography

`campus-community.webp` is an optimized local copy of the illustrative graduation photograph by **Luis Alegria**, sourced from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/graduate-in-green-gown-and-cap-before-a-university-building-YU_J19Ym0LM).

It illustrates fictional demonstration content. Neither the photographer, the person shown, nor the depicted institution is represented as a YouConect partner or user. No external image request is needed to display this demo asset.
